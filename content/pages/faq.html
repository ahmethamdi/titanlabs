Ordering & payment

Placing an order

Which payment methods do you accept?

We accept SEPA bank transfer and cryptocurrency. All payments are processed securely in euros at checkout, and your order is dispatched once payment has cleared.

Do you offer discounts for larger orders?

Yes. Our stack discount is applied automatically in the cart — no code needed:

  • 4 or more items: −5% on the whole order
  • 10 or more items: −10% on the whole order

You can mix any formats — vials, pens, sprays and orals all count toward the total. For institutional or bulk pricing beyond this, see our wholesale page.

What sizes of peptide packaging are available?

Most peptides are supplied as lyophilised powder in 10 mg vials, with selected compounds available in larger sizes such as 30 mg, 50 mg or 500 mg. Where a product offers more than one size, you can select it on the product page before adding it to your cart.

We also stock pre-filled peptide pens, nasal sprays and oral capsules for compounds where those formats are available.

Getting your order

Quality & handling

Testing and storage

Do you include test certificates with your products?

Yes. Every batch is independently tested before release, and the Certificate of Analysis is published against its batch number on our Lab Results page. Match the batch number printed on your vial to the table to open the exact certificate for what you received.

Each COA covers HPLC purity, mass-spectrometry identity confirmation, net content, and heavy metal, endotoxin and sterility screening.

How should I store the peptides upon arrival?

Our peptides are lyophilised (freeze-dried), which makes them shelf-stable for several weeks at room temperature — so a few days in transit will not affect them.

  • Long-term storage (unreconstituted): keep in a freezer at −20°C, protected from light.
  • After reconstitution: refrigerate at 2–8°C and use within the working period appropriate to the compound.

Avoid repeated freeze–thaw cycles, which degrade peptide integrity.

Are your products for human consumption?

No. All products sold by Titan Labs are supplied strictly for in-vitro laboratory research. They are not medicines, not dietary supplements, and are not intended for human or veterinary use.

Nothing on this website constitutes medical advice or a suggestion of therapeutic use. By ordering, you confirm you are a qualified purchaser using these materials for legitimate research purposes.

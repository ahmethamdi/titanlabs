What a COA proves

One certificate per batch, not per product

A Certificate of Analysis belongs to a specific batch, not to a product line. Two vials of the same peptide from different batches are different material, tested separately, with their own certificates.

That is why the batch number is printed on your vial and why the Lab Results page is searchable by batch. Match the number on the vial in your hand to the table, and you get the analysis for exactly what you received — not a representative sample from some earlier run.

Every batch we release is screened before it ships. If a batch does not meet the threshold, it is not sold.

The tests

What each analysis actually tells you

Four different questions, four different methods. A certificate that answers only the first one is not telling you very much.

HPLC purity

High-performance liquid chromatography separates the sample and measures what proportion of it is the target peptide. This is the number quoted as “≥99% purity”.

Mass spectrometry

Confirms the compound is the peptide it claims to be, by matching its molecular weight against the expected value. Purity is meaningless without identity.

Net peptide content

How much actual peptide is in the vial, as distinct from salts and residual water. A vial can be highly pure and still contain less peptide than its label implies.

Safety screening

Heavy metals, endotoxins and sterility are screened separately. These are pass/fail checks and are reported as such on the certificate.

Independence

Why third-party testing is the whole point

A manufacturer testing its own material and publishing its own result is asking you to trust the same party twice. Independent testing removes that circularity: the laboratory has no stake in whether a batch passes.

Samples are drawn from the finished, filled batch — not from the raw peptide before filling — so the certificate describes the material in the vial you receive.

If you need the analysis for a batch that is not yet listed, or documentation in a specific format for your institution, contact us with the batch number.

Questions

Documentation, answered

Is a Certificate of Analysis included with my order?

The certificate for your batch is published on the Lab Results page and stays available there. Match the batch number on your vial to find it.

Why does the purity figure differ between batches?

Because each figure is a real measurement of a specific run rather than a target copied onto every label. Batches vary slightly; all released batches meet our threshold, and the exact measured value is what appears on the certificate.

Can I verify the certificate independently?

Yes — that is the reason we publish the batch number, the test date and the methods. Once the testing laboratory and its accreditation reference are listed above, the accreditation can be checked directly with the issuing body.

Do you supply documentation for institutional purchasing?

Yes. Institutions and distributors often need documents in a particular format or with additional detail. Contact us with your requirements and the batch numbers involved, or see Wholesale & Institutional Supply.

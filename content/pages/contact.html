Before you write

Get a faster answer

Most enquiries we receive are already covered in our documentation. Checking these first usually saves you a day:

  • Lab Results — search any batch number to open its Certificate of Analysis
  • Shipping & Delivery — rates, free-shipping thresholds, delivery times and customs
  • Track Order — live status using your order number and email
  • FAQ — payment methods, storage guidance, packaging sizes
  • Refunds — what to do if something arrives damaged or incorrect

If you are writing about an existing order, include your order number and, for a batch question, the batch number printed on the vial. That lets us answer in one reply instead of three.

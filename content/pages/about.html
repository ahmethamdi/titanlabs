Our standard

Precision. Purity. Documentation.

When it comes to research quality, precision is non-negotiable. A peptide that is 95% pure is not the same compound as one that is 99% pure, and the difference shows up in your data long before it shows up on a label.

We founded Titan Labs to close that gap. Every batch is screened by accredited third-party laboratories: HPLC quantifies purity, mass spectrometry confirms peptide identity, and the full analysis is published against the batch number printed on your vial.

Nothing is self-reported. If a batch does not meet our threshold, it does not ship.

How a batch reaches you

From synthesis to your bench

  1. Synthesis & purification

    Peptides are produced to pharmaceutical-grade manufacturing standards, then purified and lyophilised for shelf stability.

  2. Independent analysis

    A sample from every batch goes to an accredited third-party laboratory for HPLC purity, mass-spec identity confirmation, and heavy metal, endotoxin and sterility screening.

  3. Certificate of Analysis published

    Results are published against the batch number and made searchable on our Lab Results page — before the batch is released for sale.

  4. Discreet dispatch

    Orders leave our German fulfilment centre in plain, protective packaging with tracking on every shipment.
